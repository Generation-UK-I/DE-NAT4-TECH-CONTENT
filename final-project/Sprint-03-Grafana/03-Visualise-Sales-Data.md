# Visualise Sales Data

## Description

Implementation:

- Volume (# products sold per time period)
- Volume (# products sold per branch)
- Revenue (per branch, per timescale)

Examples above of visualisations you could try. You can come up with any type of data visualisation so long as it's relevant to the stakeholders, please consult with your product owner on their requirements and create new user stories accordingly.

You can create as many visualisations you want until the end of the project to support your presentation finding.
