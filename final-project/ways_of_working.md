# Ways of Working – Group Projects

## Respect each other

Create an inclusive environment
Be comfortable expressing your ideas
Be respectful when discussing ideas
Give constructive feedback
Be accepting of feedback

## Start the day with a standup

This should be approximately 15 mins
Plan what is happening in the day, including who is working on what
Find out about any challenges/blockers - discuss how the team can help/solve them (note: don’t resolve these blockers during the standup, just make a POA to resolve - this could be that the affected people stay on a call after standup)

## Ask for information about tickets from Product Owners (your instructors)

Don’t guess what the requirements are
Request support/information through Slack (allows your instructors to plan and respond)

## Keep good communication

Raise questions as they come up
Make sure to ask for help when needed
There are no silly questions
Don’t leave anyone behind
Use pairing and group work
Cameras should always be on during all team meetings

## Rotating leadership role (Scrum master)

This role is a servant leadership role
Enabling decision making, but not being the sole decision maker

## Understand individual commitments

People have different personal commitments
Communicate when we can’t work 9-5
No expectation of working outside of these hours
Instructors will run the zoom from 9-4.30, available on Slack until 5
Be punctual – communicate here if you are running late or going to be absent

## Make sure to take breaks/don’t do too much

Be mindful of burning out

## Keep your commitments to the team

Communicate early if that’s not possible (it’s ok if something is more challenging!)
Everybody should be contributing to the team
If keen to spend time upskilling to support your team - add it to the trello board

## Use Tech Leads (Your instructors) when stuck

Make sure that research has been done
Work together as a team to try and work through a problem
Don’t spend too long being blocked - if you cannot move forward after a day, raise it.
Don’t hesitate to drop a message in Slack for the Tech Leads (Tag your instructor)

## Sprint Planning

Scrum master should facilitate discussion and gain agreement on ticket allocation
Ask Tech Leads to support with Sprint Planning if there are any challenges

## Retros

Retros should be supportive of each other and the needs of the team
Retro timings should try to ensure all group members are present
Ask Tech Leads to support with retros if needed
