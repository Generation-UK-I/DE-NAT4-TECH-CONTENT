# Organise team retro

## Description

Retrospectives are an important part of the agile software development lifecycle. Pausing to reflect on what went well and what didn't go so well for each sprint of work allows us to identify how we can continuously improve as a team and gives us structure around providing team feedback.

It is the project team's responsibility to own and run this agile ceremony.
Deciding on the format of the retro each week and who is going to lead it / take down actions is part of deciding you teams' ways of working.

## User Story

**As a** product owner
**I want** a meeting invite to a regularly scheduled team retro
**So That** the team knows when and where to meet
**And** we can introduce the concept of feedback and reflection into our team culture from the beginning
