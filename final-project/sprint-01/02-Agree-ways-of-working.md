# Agree ways of working

## What is 'Ways of Working / Agile Working'?

Agile working is about bringing people, processes, connectivity and technology, time and place together to find the most appropriate and effective way of working to carry out a particular task. It is working within guidelines (of the task) but without boundaries (of how you achieve it). - [The Agile Organisation](https://www.agile.org.uk/what-is-agile-working/)

## How to apply it

Ask yourselves these questions as a group:

- How are you going to operate efficiently as a team?
- Will you have a daily standup?
- Will you have retrospectives?
- How will you structure your retro?
- How will you decide to split the workload?
- What methods of communication will you use?
- Will you use different git branches to create and test features in?
- Will you make use of GitHub's Pull Request feature so that other members can review your code?

## User Story

**As a** member of this team
**I want** to create a ways of working with my team
**So that** we understand how to operate and communicate efficiently
